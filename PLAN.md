# We're Back — Full Plan

2 weeks of home rehab (exams + Lyon), then 12 weeks of gym. Gym days: Mon / Thu / Sat.

## The rules

- **Pain rule:** 0–3/10 during an exercise is fine, as long as it is back to normal by the next morning. Above 3/10 or worse the next day = lower the weight, shorten the range, or swap the exercise.
- **Progression:** finish each set with 2–3 reps left in the tank. When every set reaches the top of its rep range, add a little weight.
- **Gates:** only move to the next step when the current one feels fine. Repeating a step is progress, not failure.
- **Never skip two days of rehab in a row.** 10 minutes still counts.
- **Joints:** dumbbells, cables and machines over barbells; neutral grip; no deadlifts or barbell squats; knee tracks over the toes.
- **Stop signs:** a joint that swells, locks, gives way or feels hot, or pain that keeps getting worse or wakes you at night. Stop that movement and get it checked.
- **Goalkeeping:** no sprints, jump drills or full dives until weeks 9–12. If you have training or a match on Fri or Sun, keep Saturday's leg work light.

## The routines

**A. Home rehab (10 min)**

1. Shoulder holds, 3 × 30 s each, at 30–50% effort:
   - Push out to the side: stand sideways to a wall and press the back of your arm into it.
   - Push outward: elbow bent at 90°, push the back of your hand against a door frame.
   - Push inward: same position, push your palm against the door frame.
2. Wall sit, 3 × 30–45 s
3. Adductor squeeze, 5 × 10 s (pillow between your knees, lying down, about 50% effort)
4. Glute bridge, 2 × 12
5. Side plank on your forearm, 2 × 20–30 s each side

**B. Full home session (20 min)**

Everything in A, plus:

6. Dead bug, 3 × 8 each side
7. Side-lying leg raise, 2 × 15 each side
8. Straight-leg raise, 2 × 12 each leg
9. Bird dog, 3 × 8 each side (on your forearms while the pinky is splinted)

**C. Nightly yoga (10–12 min)**

1. Breathing on your back, 1 min (4 s in, 6 s out)
2. Cat-cow on your forearms, 8 rounds
3. Figure-4 stretch lying down, 45 s each side
4. Lying twist, 45 s each side
5. Slow glute bridge, 5 reps with a 5 s hold
6. Hamstring stretch with a towel, 45 s each leg
7. Child's pose with a pillow under your hips, 1 min
8. Legs up the wall, 2–3 min

**How it gets harder**

| Weeks | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Plan exactly as written. Groin exercise: adductor squeeze, 5 × 10 s. |
| 3–4 | Add band row (3 × 12) on Mon and Sat. Groin exercise: hip adduction machine, light, 3 × 12. Sat: goblet squat to a high bench (3 × 8–10) replaces the wall sit. |
| 5–8 | One-arm cable row (3 × 10 each) replaces the band row. Add a light neutral-grip dumbbell floor press on Mon (3 × 10). Groin exercise: Copenhagen plank, short lever, 3 × 15–20 s each side. Add step-ups on Thu (3 × 8 each leg). Walk-jogs on Tue and Fri after rehab. |
| 9–12 | More sets and weight on everything at 3/10 or lower. Jumping, sprints and dives come back (below). |

**Walk-jog (weeks 5–8, Tue and Fri)**

1. Week 5: 5 × (1 min jog, 1 min walk)
2. Week 6: 5 × (2 min jog, 1 min walk)
3. Week 7: 12–15 min easy jog
4. Week 8: jog + 4 × 40 m strides at about 70%

**Goalkeeper return (weeks 9–12, one step per week at 3/10 or lower)**

1. Week 9: pogo hops on the spot, 3 × 10; drop-and-stick landings, 3 × 5
2. Week 10: small broad jumps, 3 × 5; 4 × 10 m sprints at 80%
3. Week 11: 6 × 10 m sprints at 90%; low dives onto a soft surface
4. Week 12: full-speed sprints, full dives, full training

Do jumps and sprints at the start of a session, fresh.

## Fuel and recovery

- **Calories:** 3300–3500 a day. Weigh yourself every morning; the weekly average should rise 0.25–0.5 kg. Flat for 2 weeks = add 300 calories.
- **Protein:** 120–140 g a day, over 4 meals.
- **Easy extra calories:** milk, oats, peanut butter, rice, olive oil; a shake of milk + banana + oats + peanut butter is about 700–800 kcal.
- **Sleep:** 8+ hours.

If a joint gets worse two weeks in a row, drop back a step.
